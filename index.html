Privacy & support

ManaTally — MTG Life Tracker Privacy Policy

Public privacy contact: BearJ3rkApps@protonmail.com
Effective date: October 20, 2026

Local game information

ManaTally stores game state, player names, counters, commander damage, timers, profiles, settings, and selected images in the app's local storage. You do not need an account to use the app. ManaTally does not include advertising or analytics SDKs or a developer-operated game-data server.

Optional card artwork search

When you search for card artwork, the app sends the card name you enter to Scryfall and downloads an image from the image address returned by that service. Those services receive the request and ordinary network information such as your IP address and a ManaTally app identifier/version. Downloaded artwork is stored locally for use as your mat image. Do not enter sensitive personal information in card search.

Selected images

You can choose an image through Android's document picker. The app reads the selected image and saves it locally for your chosen background. It does not upload your selected local image to a developer server.

Optional support links

The tip-jar button opens Ko-fi in an external browser. Ko-fi and its payment providers handle any information or payment you choose to submit under their own policies. ManaTally does not receive or process your payment-card details. Supporting the app does not unlock in-app features.

Backups and deletion

The app includes manual backup export and restore. You control where you copy or share exported backups; backups may contain player names, game information, and images. Android backup is enabled in the manifest, so device/system backup and restore behavior may also apply depending on your Android version and settings.

You can clear app customizations and saved app data using Restore Defaults in Settings, or clear the app's storage through Android Settings. Uninstalling removes local app data; retained exported backups and device/cloud backups must be managed separately using the service or location where they were saved. The developer does not have a remote copy of your game to delete.

Google Play updates

The Play distribution uses Google Play for app delivery and updates. It does not use the app's GitHub APK updater. Google processes store and update interactions according to its own policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when app data practices change. For privacy or support questions, contact BearJ3rkApps@protonmail.com.
